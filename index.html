🏛️ How Goa is governed locally

Rural: Village Panchayats serve local rural communities; North Goa and South Goa each have a Zilla Panchayat at district level. Each Zilla Panchayat has 25 electoral constituencies, not 25 separate Zilla Panchayats.

Urban: Municipal Councils serve towns; the Corporation of the City of Panaji serves the city.

Source: Goa State Election Commission.
